Pakistan's Bitcoin And Crypto Trading Sector Faces Religious Scrutiny Following Scholar's Fatwa On Digital Assets | Crowdfund Insider

Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani issued a religious decree through Darul Uloom Karachi classifying cryptocurrency trading and investment as impermissible under Shariah law. The ruling explicitly includes stablecoins such as USDT alongside major cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum, asserting they fail to satisfy core Shariah criteria for legitimate wealth or property. Rebranding these assets as "virtual currency" does not alter their prohibited status. This fatwa arrived amid Pakistan's establishment of the Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA), which aims to license exchanges, curb illicit activities, and integrate blockchain into the financial ecosystem. Officials emphasize consumer protection and risk mitigation while exploring opportunities for tokenized assets.
